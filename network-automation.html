Patents in Network Automation

Patented technologies covering closed-loop RAN optimization, spectrum and power management, ML-based parameter control, and automated remediation.

PatentTechnologySON relationship
US 9,408,210 — “Method, Device and System for Dynamic Frequency Spectrum Optimization”Predicts cell traffic, generates alternative spectrum allocations, evaluates them against multiple network KPIs, and automatically chooses an improved allocation. Inventors: Pikhletsky, Luo, Zhuang, Khafizov; Huawei.Very strong SON self-optimization patent. It automatically adapts spectrum allocation based on changing network conditions. (Justia Patents ↗)
US 8,665,690 — “System and Method for Uplink Power Control in a Wireless Communications System”Dynamically computes uplink power offsets and power-control commands based on measured received power and target values. Inventors include Farid Khafizov.Radio-resource / interference optimization closely associated with SON optimization. (Justia Patents ↗)
US 11,329,737 / US 11,716,161 — “Systems and Methods for Modification of Radio Access Network Parameters Based on Channel Propagation Models Generated Using Machine Learning Techniques”Uses AI/ML to model sectors and channel propagation/interference and then determines actions that modify RAN configuration parameters. Inventors: Farid Khafizov, Mark Newbury, Venkata Dhulipalla; Verizon.This is essentially AI-driven SON: measure/model network conditions → determine optimization → modify RAN parameters. (Justia Patents ↗)
US 11,924,778 / US 12,213,083 — “Method and Apparatus for Optimizing RAN Power Consumption”Uses UE field measurements, predicted signal strength, cell boundaries, overlap, and ML-based models to determine optimized cell boundaries while meeting service requirements. Khafizov & Newbury, Verizon.Strong example of closed-loop RAN self-optimization, particularly energy-saving / coverage optimization. (Justia Patents ↗)
US 12,621,692 — “Tracking User Equipment Dynamics in Wireless Networks”Detects a base-station outage, tracks where affected UEs move, identifies users without service, and can update cellular-network operating parameters. Khafizov & Ibraheem, Verizon.Fits SON self-healing / outage compensation particularly well. (Justia Patents ↗)
US 2024/0365234 — “Systems and Methods for Band-Based Power Saving in a Wireless Network”Examines hand-ins by RF band, ranks bands, and selectively applies power-saving behavior at the base station.
Inventors: Chin Chiu, Sachin Vargantwar, Farid Khafizov, Asif Dawoodi Gandhi		SON-style automated RAN energy optimization. (Justia Patents ↗)
US 12,659,246 — “User Equipment Impact Estimation and Remediation”Detects network-device failure, uses energy measurements and KPI changes to quantify UE impact, then performs a remedial network action. Khafizov & Safin.Very close to SON self-healing: detect → quantify → remediate.
